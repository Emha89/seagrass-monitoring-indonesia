Estimate carbon for your own area.

Draw a shape anywhere on the map below and this tool calculates an above-ground carbon estimate for that exact area, along with a trend forecast through 2024.

AGC calculator

How to use this tool

  1. Zoom to your area. Use the search box or the map controls to navigate to the location you want to check.
  2. Draw your polygon. Click "Draw AOI", then click points on the map to trace a shape, and click the first point again to close it.
  3. Read the results. The panel on the right shows the estimated carbon total and a trend forecast for the area you drew.

For reference, Indonesia's national annual total sits between about 169,000 and 194,000 tonnes of carbon (see the national chart), so you can compare your own area against the national picture. Estimates are modelled and best read as an indication, not a survey-grade measurement.

About this research

These tools are part of a PhD thesis on satellite-based seagrass carbon estimation in Indonesia, at the University of Queensland in partnership with BRIN. Estimates are modelled from field surveys and satellite imagery, and are still being extended and validated in more regions.
