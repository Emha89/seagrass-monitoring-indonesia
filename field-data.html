Help expand the field data behind this model.

This model is only as good as the field data behind it. More surveys, especially from under-sampled regions and years, directly improve the accuracy of every estimate on this site. If you collect seagrass field data in Indonesia, here is how to contribute it.

What data helps most

New field surveys are most useful when they follow a consistent, non-destructive format:

Location
Latitude and longitude for each quadrat.
Species
Which seagrass species are present at each quadrat.
Percent cover
A visual, non-destructive estimate per species, from 0 to 100 percent.
Shoot density
Shoot counts per species within a known quadrat area, commonly 25 × 25 cm or 50 × 50 cm.
Layout
At least 3 to 5 quadrats placed within a single 10 × 10 metre area, matching one satellite pixel.
Survey date
The date each quadrat was recorded.

How your data is used

Your quadrat survey is combined with an existing species-level carbon reference collection to produce a pixel-representative carbon value, which then becomes new training data for the national model.
