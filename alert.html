Pilot project

Flagging where seagrass may have disappeared.

For Kaledupa island, this tool compares each season against nine years of history to flag spots where seagrass used to reliably grow but currently does not. It complements the AGC calculator by pointing to where change may be happening, rather than how much carbon an area holds.

About this tool

For each three-month season, this tool classifies every location in Kaledupa island as seagrass present or absent, then compares that against how often the same season was classified present over the previous nine years (2017–2025). A spot that is usually present but currently absent is flagged as a provisional alert. If the same drop also shows up in the season before, it becomes a confirmed alert.

This is a pilot. The change-detection logic has not yet been fully validated by research, so flagged pixels are best treated as leads worth checking on the ground, not confirmed facts. An empty confirmed-alert result does not necessarily mean an area is safe, either. Confirmed alerts need clear satellite images in two seasons in a row, which can be hard to get during the rainy season.

The underlying model was trained on presence and absence data from across Indonesia, but this app currently applies it only to Kaledupa island, to keep things fast. If you have a specific area elsewhere in Indonesia you would like checked, get in touch and it can be run for you, it also helps check the results against real local knowledge.

Change alert map

  1. Check the current status. Click "Check current status" to see the most recently completed season, worked out automatically from today's date.
  2. Or explore past seasons. Pick a Year and Season, then click "Analyze this season" to check a specific past period.
  3. Check for a confirmed pattern. Click "Check consistency with previous season" to see whether a drop also showed up the season before, which makes it a confirmed alert rather than just provisional.
  4. Turn on the alert layers. Switch on "Provisional alert" or "Confirmed alert" on the map to see flagged locations. "Baseline rate" shows how often each spot was historically present. It can take a little while to load, since it's checking nine years of imagery.

Model accuracy is about 87%, based on validation across Indonesia-wide field data, not specific to Kaledupa island. Results shown under "Explore Historical Seasons" describe that one past season only, not current, ongoing status.

About this research

These tools are part of a PhD thesis on satellite-based seagrass carbon estimation in Indonesia, at the University of Queensland in partnership with BRIN. Estimates are modelled from field surveys and satellite imagery, and are still being extended and validated in more regions.
